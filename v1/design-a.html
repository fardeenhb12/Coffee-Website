What to judge here

The type pairing (Archivo + IBM Plex Mono), the rule weight, and whether the ticket in Option 1 feels like a roastery's paperwork rather than a pricing widget. Option 2 keeps the date but gives it the full width — calmer, less information-dense.

The lot card is the part that has to survive seven of them on one screen at 390px. Note the sold-out card: it still shows a roast date and a next-roast date, because the brand's promise is that a date is never hidden.

Nothing here uses a photograph, a shadow, a rounded corner or a gradient. That is deliberate — every one of those would be borrowed from a café, and we are not one.
