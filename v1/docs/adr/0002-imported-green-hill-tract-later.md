# Launch on imported green; Bangladeshi hill-tract is chapter two

**Status**: accepted

Dhaka's clearest competitive gap is that nobody sells Bangladeshi hill-tract coffee as a
brand — East Bengal has already publicly called Chimbuk, Bandarban "the Darjeeling of
Bangladesh" and built nothing on it. It is also the strongest story available, and we are
deliberately not launching with it: farm relationships in the hill tracts take seasons,
while a roastery buying imported green can ship in weeks.

**Consequences**: the launch price band is ৳1,035–1,300, where the Dhaka market already
sits, not the ৳1,650–2,850 the previous site displayed. Hill-tract lots launch later as a
named second chapter with real farmer names and photography, not as a claim made today.
