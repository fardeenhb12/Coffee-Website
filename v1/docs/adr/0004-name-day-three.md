# The roastery is called Day Three

**Status**: proposed — pending `.com` / `.com.bd` availability and a trademark search in
Bangladesh

Coffee peaks roughly three to ten days after roasting, so the name states the product
promise rather than describing the company. It makes "the roast date is printed on every
bag" feel like the premise of the brand instead of a feature bolted onto it, and it is the
only candidate that turns the market's unclaimed differentiator into the name itself.

**Considered Options**: **Adda Coffee Roasters** — warmer and more local, but the story
leans social rather than on the one proof no Dhaka competitor prints; **Chimbuk Coffee
Co.** — contingent on hill-tract lots we are not launching with (ADR-0002).
