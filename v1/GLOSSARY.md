# Day Three

A Dhaka specialty coffee roastery selling roasted single-origin lots to home brewers.
This vocabulary exists so that page copy, code identifiers and bag printing all name the
same thing the same way.

## Customers

**Home brewer**:
The primary customer — a Dhaka resident who owns a V60, AeroPress, moka pot or French
press and buys roasted beans to brew at home.
_Avoid_: Consumer, user, customer, coffee lover, enthusiast

**Tasting room**:
The physical space where brewed coffee is served and cupping sessions are held. The
secondary path for someone who is not yet buying bags.
_Avoid_: Café, shop, store, outlet, lab

## Product

**Lot**:
One green coffee bought from a single producer or region, roasted and sold under one
name. A lot is not a blend, and it is not a SKU.
_Avoid_: SKU, product, item, bean, offering

**Bag**:
The sealed retail unit of roasted coffee, always stamped with its roast date.
_Avoid_: Pack, pouch, unit, canister

**Roast date**:
The calendar date on which a bag finished roasting. Printed on every bag and shown on
every lot; never approximated, never replaced by a dispatch estimate.
_Avoid_: Freshness date, best before, packed date, batch date

**Grind**:
The particle size a bag is ground to, chosen by brewing method at the moment of order.
_Avoid_: Grind type, calibration, setting

## Ordering

**Order request**:
What the cart submits. Confirmed by a human over WhatsApp; nothing is charged on the
site.
_Avoid_: Checkout, purchase, order, transaction, booking

**Dispatch**:
Handing a confirmed order to the courier. Same-day dispatch means it leaves the roastery
on the day the order is confirmed.
_Avoid_: Shipping, delivery, fulfilment
