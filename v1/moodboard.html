Three directions.
No photography.

Every coffee site in Dhaka looks like a café — warm brown, a serif, a latte photo. We have no photos and a claim that is administrative: a date, a lot, a producer. So the design should look like the roastery's paperwork, not its showroom.

A

Batch Sheet

The page is a batch ticket. Dates, lot numbers and producer names are the largest things on the screen, because they are the proof.

Where I'd go

Direction A. It's the only one where the brand's claim is also the visual system: if the date is the product, the date should be the largest thing on the page. B is the safe choice and the most crowded. C is the most elegant and the least ownable.

Keep from the current design: the paper palette, brass, and the motion tokens. Change: display serif → grotesque plus mono, decorative spacing → rules and data rows.

If A is chosen, the palette becomes new :root tokens in styles/main.css and DESIGN.md gains a direction section — a deliberate replacement, not an addition.
