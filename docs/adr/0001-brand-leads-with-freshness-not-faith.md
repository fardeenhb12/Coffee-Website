# Brand leads with freshness, not with a faith mission

**Status**: accepted

The site carried an explicit faith-mission identity — 100% of net proceeds to
church outreach, produce bought from family farms, an overseas non-profit partner named in
the footer. That was a deliberate position, not an oversight, and it was the only
distinctive thing on the page. We removed it. A roastery importing green coffee cannot
evidence a claim about where its proceeds go, and the framing excludes most of the Dhaka
market before the first scroll. The brand's moral claim is now checkable instead of
asserted: every bag carries its roast date, every lot names its producer.

**Considered Options**: keep the mission as the hero (polarising, unevidenced); demote it
to a quiet section (still an unevidenced claim sitting on the page); replace its object
with Bangladeshi growers (strongest story, but see ADR-0002).
