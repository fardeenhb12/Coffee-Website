The Roaster · Banani, Dhaka

Zarif Rahman Head Roaster & Founder

Most coffee in Dhaka is roasted dark to mask old crop or long sea transit. When beans are scorched dark, every origin tastes like the roaster’s drum. We roast on a cast-iron drum in Banani to do the opposite: develop sweetness while preserving the bright acidity, floral sugars, and distinct fruit notes that the producer worked a whole season to grow.

“If we roast it dark, you taste our machine. If we roast it right, you taste the soil of Guji or the altitude of Matagalpa. And if you brew it on Day Three, you taste it at its absolute peak.”

— Zarif Rahman, Head Roaster

Brew Guides

Four methods, tested on our weekly roasts. Start with these parameters, then adjust by taste.

local_cafe

V60 Pour Over

Clean · High clarity
Dose 15g
Water 250g · 93°C (1:16.6)
Grind Medium-fine (table salt)
Time 2:45 – 3:15 min

Adjust: If the cup tastes sour or thin, grind finer or pour slower; if it tastes bitter or astringent, grind coarser.

compress

AeroPress

Sweet · Full texture
Dose 16g
Water 200g · 90°C (1:12.5)
Grind Medium-fine
Time 2:00 min total

Adjust: If the brew tastes sharp or weak, steep 30 seconds longer; if astringent or difficult to press, grind coarser.

coffee_maker

French Press

Heavy body · Rich
Dose 30g
Water 500g · 95°C (1:16.6)
Grind Coarse (sea salt)
Time 4:00 – 5:00 min

Adjust: If the body feels watery, steep a minute longer; if muddy or bitter, skim the crust before plunging.

local_fire_department

Moka Pot

Intense · Espresso style
Dose 18g (level basket, no tamp)
Water 180g (pre-heated to valve)
Grind Fine (fine sand)
Time 1:30 – 2:00 min

Adjust: If the coffee tastes bitter or scorched, pull off heat immediately when sputtering begins; if sour, start with boiling water in the base.

Clear Answers · Dhaka Roastery

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about roast dates, grinds, delivery, and how we trade.

How fresh is this coffee, and when should I drink it? expand_more

Every lot is roasted in Banani on Tuesday mornings. Freshly roasted coffee releases CO₂ and needs 48 to 72 hours to settle. Your bag enters its peak flavour window on Day Three (Friday) and stays sweet and expressive through Day 14.

Can you grind the beans for my specific brewer? expand_more

Yes. Select Whole Bean, V60 / Pour Over, AeroPress, French Press, or Moka Pot when choosing your bag. We grind on commercial precision flat burrs immediately before dispatch.

How long does delivery take across Dhaka? expand_more

Orders confirmed on WhatsApp by 2:00 PM Tuesday are dispatched the same afternoon. Courier deliveries across Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi, Uttara, and greater Dhaka metro typically arrive within 24 to 48 hours. Delivery is free for orders over ৳2,500.

How do I pay for my order? expand_more

We do not take payments through the website. Submitting your bag creates a prefilled WhatsApp message with your items, grinds, and subtotal. A human confirms availability and shares payment options: bKash, Nagad, bank transfer, or cash on delivery.

Why is a lot sold out, and when will it return? expand_more

We roast estate coffees in micro-batches. When a lot sells out, its card displays the next roast date so you know when the next batch is planned. If the green crop is finished, we retire the lot and introduce a new harvest.

Do you supply cafes, creative studios, and offices? expand_more

Yes. We provide weekly batch roasts, barista calibration, and custom delivery schedules for specialty cafes and workspaces in Dhaka. Contact us via the wholesale enquiry link in the footer below.
